Project Site Plan

Site Name

Calvin Solar Pool Heat Panels

This name clearly identifies the business and services being offered.The company focuses on renewable energy solutions for residential and commercial swimming pools.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote and provide information about solar pool heating systems for customers. The site will educate poolowners about the benefits of solar energy. It will provide a quote request form where customers can submit their pool dimensions and contact information.

Scenarios

  • How much do solar pool heat panels cost?
  • How many panels do my swimming pool require
  • What are the benefits of using solar pool heating system?
  • How Long does the panels last?
